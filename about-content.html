Our Mission

Mirror Lake Retreat serves the Christian community by providing a year-round center for education, spiritual renewal, relaxation and recreation through retreats, encounters, conference, and camps in a Christ-centered resort atmosphere.

Religious Mission and Facility Use Policy

Mirror Lake Retreat is a religious nonprofit ministry established and operated in furtherance of its Christian mission and in accordance with its Statement of Faith. All use of the Center's property, facilities, and resources must be consistent with and supportive of that mission.

Mirror Lake reserves the right to determine, in its sole discretion and consistent with applicable law, which organizations, groups, and activities may utilize its facilities. Facility use is a privilege and not an entitlement.

As a ministry committed to historic Christian orthodoxy, the Center makes its facilities available only to Christian churches, ministries, and organizations whose foundational beliefs and practices are consistent with the essential doctrines of the historic Christian faith as articulated in the Center's Statement of Faith.

Accordingly, Mirror Lake does not make its facilities available for use by religious organizations, ministries, or groups whose foundational theological doctrines materially conflict with or depart from those essential Christian teachings. This includes organizations whose teachings reject or materially depart from the historic Christian understanding of the nature of God, the person and divinity of Jesus Christ, the Trinity, and the authority of Scripture.

All prospective groups and organizations are subject to review and approval by Mirror Lake’s administration. Mirror Lake reserves the right to deny, restrict, or discontinue facility use when a proposed use, sponsoring organization's beliefs, or associated activities are inconsistent with Mirror Lake’s religious mission, Statement of Faith, or established facility-use policies, subject to applicable law.

Nothing in this policy shall be construed as a judgment concerning the personal worth or dignity of any individual. This policy governs Mirror Lake’s institutional affiliations, ministry partnerships, and use of its property in furtherance of its religious purpose.

Statement of Faith

Mirror Lake Retreat is owned and operated by the Metro New York District Church of the Nazarene

We believe in one God-the Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

We believe that the Old and New Testament Scriptures, given by plenary inspiration, contain all truth necessary to faith and Christian living.

We believe that man is born with a fallen nature, and is, therefore, inclined to evil.

We believe that the finally impenitent are hopelessly and endlessly lost.

We believe that the atonement through Jesus Christ is for the whole human race; and that whosoever repents and believes on the Lord Jesus Christ is justified and regenerated and saved from the dominion of sin.

We believe that believers are to be sanctified wholly, subsequent to regeneration, through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ.

We believe that the Holy Spirit bears witness to the new birth, and also to the entire sanctification of believers.

We believe that our Lord will return, the dead will be raised, and the final judgement will take place.
